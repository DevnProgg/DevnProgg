A Brief Introduction

I’m a software engineer based in Southern Africa with a strong focus on building practical, scalable systems for real-world problems, especially in financial technology and intelligent systems. I currently design and build loan management systems with features like automatic billing, payment tracking, and data-driven decision support, targeting environments with limited hardware resources and intermittent connectivity. My work prioritizes performance, simplicity, and reliability, making technology accessible even on low-end devices. My technical background spans React-based web applications, RESTful APIs, and backend systems in JavaScript and Python, with growing specialization in high-performance services using Go. I also work with desktop applications using Tauri + React, enabling offline-first solutions with SQLite. Beyond fintech, I’m actively exploring AI, computer vision, and data-driven automation, including projects involving vehicle detection, intelligent chatbots, and predictive systems. I’m particularly interested in how AI can augment — not replace — human work. I’m on a long-term path toward becoming a software architect, with a strong interest in system design, scalability, security, and cloud-native architectures. I enjoy building things from scratch, learning deeply, and turning ideas into production-ready systems that create measurable impact.
